Is it for you?

No BS. Here's the deal.

PaulSklarXFit365 is serious training for men who are ready to put the time in. Here's who it's built for.

It's for you if

  • You're committed and ready to train consistently
  • You can train 5 days a week for 60 to 90 minutes (the suggested schedule)
  • You have a gym: barbell, dumbbells, bench, pull-up bar, suspension trainer, and bands or a cable station
  • You're intermediate or advanced, or a beginner willing to scale it down

It's not for you if

  • You want a plan written just for you. This isn't personal training
  • You want a quick 12-week shred. This is how Paul trains all year
  • You need extensive modifications, for example around an injury

Questions

Before you join.

How do I get the app?

PaulSklarXFit365 is a private app, so it isn't in the App Store. Join at checkout and you get access to the app afterwards.

What equipment do I need?

A barbell, dumbbells, weight plates, an adjustable bench, a pull-up bar, a suspension trainer, and resistance bands and/or a cable station. Kettlebells, a squat rack, a hex bar, a battle rope, slam balls and a jump rope are highly recommended. It's built for a gym or studio.

How long are the workouts?

Usually 60 to 90 minutes, depending on your experience and setup. The suggested schedule is 5 training days and 2 rest days a week, and plenty of members adapt it to their week.

I'm not advanced. Can I do it?

The program is geared toward intermediate and advanced trainees, and it can be scaled for beginners. Every exercise has a video demo so you can see how it's done.

Is it personalized?

No. Everyone follows the same program. It isn't personal training, so if you need a plan built around you or extensive modifications, it may not be the right fit.

What's in the nutrition guidelines?

Paul's eating strategies and mindset, his protein intake, the foods he eats and avoids, a sample day of eating, and what he does before and after training. It's not a personalized meal plan.
